Overview

Manali is a mountain town in Himachal Pradesh, known for its snow-capped peaks, pine forests, and adventure sports.

Nature

From the Solang Valley to the Rohtang Pass, Manali offers some of the most breathtaking natural landscapes in India.

Culture

The town blends Himachali tradition with a vibrant backpacker culture, visible in its temples, cafes, and local markets.

Travel Tips

Visit between March and June for pleasant weather, or in winter for snow activities. Carry warm clothing year-round.
